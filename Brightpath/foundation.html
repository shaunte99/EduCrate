What is the Foundation Phase?

The Foundation Phase focuses on essential early learning skills for children in Grades 1 to 3. At BrightPath we combine fun activities with structured lessons to build reading, counting and social skills. Our tutors use small group and one on one sessions so each learner gets the attention they need.
